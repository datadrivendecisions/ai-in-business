About this handbook

Who writes it. Students of HAN University of Applied Sciences, in teams, as the integrated task of the AIBS module (the research) and the AEL module (the platform it is researched and written with). Each cohort adds new chapters and refreshes existing ones, so the handbook compounds over time rather than being written from zero.

The quality bar. Every chapter is measured against the same four criteria before it is published: clarity for a non-technical reader, accuracy against appraised sources, responsible-AI coverage, and usefulness to an SME owner. Nothing reaches an SME reader unreviewed — chapters are curated by the course team before publication.

Educational, not consultancy. This handbook offers inspiration and starting points, not commercial advice or guarantees. Every chapter carries this disclaimer. Decisions about a firm remain the firm's own.

Confidentiality. Real SME cases are anonymised or published only with consent. Sensitive or identifying data never enters the public handbook.
