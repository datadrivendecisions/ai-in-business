The source menu

Two rounds, two claims. In each one you write down where you stand, then you are shown sixteen sources one at a time and may open eight of them. In one of the two rounds an assistant asks you about your position first.

Before you start

This is a class exercise about how people look for evidence. It records which sources you chose to open, the order you saw them in, how long you spent, and how the round felt. You can stop at any point and erase everything.
