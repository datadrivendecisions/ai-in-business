On the day

  • A pitch of about five minutes. Built around the guiding questions below. Keep it short: most of the 20 minutes is for our questions.
  • Then our questions, to each of you. We ask each of you in turn. Know what is in your own portfolio: if it says you did something, expect to be asked how.

The mini-CBI in week 4 did not count towards your mark. It showed us where you stand, and you where to go next.

The guiding questions

Prepare all of them. Your pitch can pick its focus; our questions will come back to every one.

1 · Research

Take us from your SME's problem to your answer. What did you want to know, and what did you find out? Based on your desk and field research, what would you recommend to SMEs in the field you explored?

2 · Judgement

This was the first time you did research with AI. What changed? Did it change how you engage with your research data, for better, for worse, or both?

And concretely: how much do you trust what your tool produced? What did you do to monitor and improve the quality of its output?

3 · Perspective

a. What have you learned about the business context your SME, or sector of SMEs, operates in? And especially: how is AI changing that context?

b. Look at what you found in your research, and at how you worked in this course. How do you see your own future job, and your role as a business professional? How might AI change the business field, and individual jobs?

4 · Growth

What can you do now that you could not do in week 1? Which feedback changed your work most — the AI tutor, your peer team, or the mini-CBI — and how?
