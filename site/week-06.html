AIBS — the economy we build with AI

Last week each team looked at front-runners in its own sector, in China, the United States or Europe. This week each team pitches its postcard from 2035. Then we discuss a set of statements on how the choices we make now shape the economy, and the jobs that come with AI. Take a side, and give a reason from your own case.

This discussion is a first rehearsal for guiding question 3 of the final CBI.
