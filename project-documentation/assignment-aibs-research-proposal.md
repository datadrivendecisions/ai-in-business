# AIBS — Week 1 assignment: research proposal (first draft)

*Minimal prompt for the first iteration. To be elaborated later if we want.*

Write a **first-draft research proposal** for your team's handbook — the handbook about the state of AI that the tool you build in AEL will produce. Keep it to **1–3 pages**. Cover:

1. **Target audience** — which SMEs is this handbook for? Be as specific as you can: sector, size, region. A named partner company is ideal.
2. **What they need** — what do you think these SMEs need to know or decide about AI, and why? Use the Draghi report and your own reasoning.
3. **Research question** — one **main question** plus **at least 4 sub-questions** that together answer "what do these SMEs need to know about AI?" At least two sub-questions for the **external analysis** (the SME's context, market, and risks) and at least two for the **internal analysis** (this SME's own situation, capabilities, and needs).
4. **Quality criteria for the handbook** — what must the handbook do to be genuinely useful to this audience? This list becomes the checklist you will later use to judge it.
5. **Methodology** — how you'll answer the questions: your **desk research** approach and your **field research** approach (one SME visit in weeks 2–4). Which questions does each method serve?

This is a starting point. You will revise it as you learn more.

**Hand in:** one document per team, [where], by [when].
**The bar:** a reader can tell who the handbook serves, what it must answer, and how you'll judge whether it's any good.
