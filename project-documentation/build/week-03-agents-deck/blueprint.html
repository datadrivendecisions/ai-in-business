Blueprint: the AI agents segment

Three cards, three notes, one provenance table — and the line the segment may not cross before the experiment has run.

The three-card segment on AI agents in the week 3 session: what goes on each card, what the lecturer says over it, where every claim comes from, and the two skill checks every card passes.

This document describes the finished segment only. The order of work and the record of what exists belong to the build plan, which is the state. When the two disagree, this document is the intent and the card page is the backlog.

2 · The goal

Three cards introduce AI agents to the 32 students of week 3 in at most 15 minutes, after the experiment debrief, each card inside the slide-deck limits, every factual claim traced to an AI Wiki page, no AI-language finding left unreviewed, and the presenter deck, PDF and PPTX produced by one command.

Every rule below serves a part of that sentence, and every part of it is covered by a rule. The trace is in §8.

4 · Where the segment sits in the session

experiment round 1 ─ experiment round 2 ─ DEBRIEF ─┬─ card 1  an agent is what you already use
                                                   ├─ card 2  the harness is the part you own
                                                   └─ card 3  trust splits three ways
                                                        │
                                                   team work on the week-3 page

The order is load-bearing. The experiment measures how an agent shapes what a student reads, and its protocol keeps the hypothesis direction out of the room until the debrief. A segment on agents placed before it would be a treatment nobody randomised. Placed after, card 3 can point at the round every student has just done.

7 · What the checks enforce

check-agents-deck.py --spec checks this document: the ten sections in order, an anchor for every rule, a level, a criterion with a number and a unit, a method from the four allowed, one shall per rule, no vague terms, and not every rule a Must. check-agents-deck.py --deck checks every rule whose method is Test against the card page, the notes and the provenance table. It runs slidewords.py’s measurement for SL-2 to SL-5 and aiprose.py for PR-2 to PR-4, so the two skills are the checks and not a copy of them.

Two additions to the skills, both local to this check. The skill’s JARGON list carries business terms and none of this segment’s, so PR-3 adds its own: harness, LLM, RAG, SLM, MCP, LLM Wiki, Fat Skills, agentic, context window, ReAct. Each counts as explained when the skill’s own is_explained() accepts a line on the card where it first appears; the card is the unit because a headline has no room for a gloss. And the check ends every headline, bullet and paragraph with a full stop before handing it to aiprose.py. The script marks a block boundary with the character \x1e, which Python’s \s matches, so without the full stop a headline ending …the harness runs into a bullet starting The harness and A5 reports harness the.

The Inspection and Demonstration rules have no script. They are performed by a person as part of the build plan’s tester steps, and recorded in the worklog checks register.

9 · Deliberate exclusions

  • Won’t: embedded images. The decks have no image pipeline; the visualisations are opened live from their links.
  • Won’t: the three later harness layers explained. The LRD makes them debrief reading for weeks 4 to 6.
  • Won’t: a rule on em-dash density (A10). Em dashes are house style in this repository.
  • Won’t: D-codes on the notes. The notes are read by the lecturer, and the D-codes encode a student reader.
  • Won’t: a Dutch version.

10 · Open questions

  • The card order around the debrief. SE-1 needs a card whose kicker is Debrief. If the week 3 deck marks the debrief differently, SE-1’s criterion changes with it. Owner: the AIBS owner.
  • Whether the card page may show the segment before the session. SE-2 makes an early reader harmless; publishing the segment only after the session would make SE-2 redundant. Owner: both module owners.
