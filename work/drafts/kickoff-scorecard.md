# Session scorecard — didactic quality

*A scoring instrument derived from the LRD's Part 2 (Bloom, Motivation & Flow, UnBlooms,
Durable skills) plus the session craft those axioms imply. Built to score the first joint
session; reusable for any contact block. Scores are self-assessed and therefore optimistic:
the second module owner should score independently before the two are compared — the same
rule the LRD sets for the interview (AC-12).*

## Scale

Each item scores 0–3.

- **3** — designed in: a named mechanism in the programme, and a way to see it happened.
- **2** — present but partial: the mechanism is there for some students or some of the time.
- **1** — mentioned: the programme says it, nothing makes it happen.
- **0** — absent.

Eighteen items, maximum 54. Target: above 90 %, i.e. 49 or more.

## Items

**A · Bloom (LRD 2.1) — precision in what we ask for**

- A1. Each block states the cognitive level it asks for as a verb plus an object, and the
  session's arc reaches Evaluate and Create.
- A2. Students do their own Analyse and Evaluate work; the AI reaching Create does not stand
  in for it.
- A3. Metacognitive knowledge: a structured moment where each student reflects on their own
  process and records it.

**B · Motivation and Flow (LRD 2.2) — autonomy, mastery, purpose; informational feedback**

- B1. Autonomy: every team makes at least two real choices that shape its work.
- B2. Mastery and the flow channel: every task has a visible finish line inside its block, the
  challenge is calibrated to week 1, and the no-coding majority has a ramp — nobody is a
  spectator of the tool.
- B3. Purpose: what students make is real (it enters the handbook repository or the platform),
  and the "why" is a concrete human, not an abstraction.
- B4. Feedback is informational, never controlling: no scores, no verdicts, no winners, from
  any source in the room including the lecturers.
- B5. Flow in the room: no lecture stretch over ten minutes, input alternates with action, no
  block leaves most students passive, movement and a break are placed.
- B6. The challenge-based arc Engage → Investigate → Act is visible in the session's shape.

**C · UnBlooms (LRD 2.3) — grade the thinking, not the output**

- C1. All four stages — Questioning, Generating, Critiquing, Refining — are experienced in
  order within the session.
- C2. AI stays on the sidelines with productive friction: the AI generates, humans question
  and critique, and Socratic questioning is present.
- C3. The thinking is made visible as an artefact (questions, failure log, revisions) and that
  artefact, not the output, is what is carried forward.

**D · Durable skills (LRD 2.4) — the capability being protected**

- D1. Collaboration is structurally required: interdependent roles, a partner team.
- D2. Creativity: an open-ended task with no single right answer and an invitation to surprise.
- D3. Critical thinking, including both AI-supported exploration and AI-supported verification,
  practised as distinct acts.

**E · Session craft (implied by 2.2 and the LRD's KISS rule, Part 5)**

- E1. Signal to noise: administrative content takes under five minutes of speech; everything
  administrative is on paper.
- E2. Curiosity: within three minutes students are inside a question or an action; the weeks
  ahead are presented as questions, not a syllabus.
- E3. Runnable: two lecturers can run it with under a minute of decision per team, and nothing
  depends on infrastructure that is not ready.

independent score by the other module owner is the one that counts.

### Cycle 4 — the trust exercise added as block 3

Change: a new block between the crew and the interview. Each student, alone, has their own
tool write one page on whether the United States, China, Europe or their home country
dominates AI; odd teams get a leading wording, even teams a neutral one, without being told.
Pairs compare within the team, then the same region is compared across the partner pair, so
the leading and neutral pages meet. The block doubles as tool setup for every student, which
pays for its twenty-two minutes: the developers' parallel track is gone, the interview is
sixteen minutes instead of twenty, and the audit and gallery lose two minutes each.
