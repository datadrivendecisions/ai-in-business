The reveal, standing, two minutes

Say the withholding plainly, first: "Half of you were given a different wording, and we did not tell you. That was the experiment, and this is the result." Then:

Same question, four confident answers. Same region, two wordings, two conclusions. And on the one page each of you could actually check — your own country — it was wrong somewhere. Now look at the back of your card. Every one of these pages was written by a tool owned by somebody, somewhere. That is the intern you are proposing to your SME: it can assert everything about AI and check nothing about them. You are in exactly the reverse position, and this week's page is what you do with that.

The bank, during the pair work — ask, never comment

  1. Point at one sentence nobody read before accepting it.
  2. Which number here could you verify right now? Which could you not?
  3. Whose tool wrote this, and what changes if its home country changes its mind?
  4. Which of these four pages would you put in front of your SME, and why not the others?

Never "good", never "yes", never "you might". If asked "is this right?", ask question 2.

Say the data rule once, from the team card, before the tools come out.
